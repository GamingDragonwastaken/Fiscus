## Needs a commercial license

Using Fiscus for a business: inside a company, for paid client work, or as
part of a product or service you sell. That includes using it to manage your
company's own AI spend.

## Getting a commercial license

Commercial licenses are deliberately cheap. The point is to keep the project
sustainable, not to put a toll booth in front of it.

1. **GitHub Sponsors.** Sponsor the project at the **Commercial** tier from the
   Sponsor button on the repository page. The maintainer then confirms your
   license in writing for the organization named in your sponsorship.
2. **Anything else** (invoicing, a fixed-term license, a larger deployment,
   or a question about whether your use counts as commercial): open a
   [discussion](https://github.com/GamingDragonwastaken/Fiscus/discussions)
   and it will be moved to private contact.

A commercial license covers the organization, not a seat count.
